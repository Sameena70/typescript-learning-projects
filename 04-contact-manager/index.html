🌟 Key Features

  • Directory: Search contacts in real-time by name, email, phone, or company.
  • Favorites: Click the gold star on any contact card to bookmark for quick access.
  • Groups: Filter contacts by Work, Clients, Business, Family, or Friends.
  • Import & Export: Backup your full contact book to JSON or spreadsheet CSV anytime.
  • View Toggle: Easily switch between modern 3-column Card Grid and structured List Table.
